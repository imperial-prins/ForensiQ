# 19 — Product Differentiation

**Status:** implementation-backed positioning

ForensiQ is differentiated by integrating ordinary-user protection and analyst investigation around one evidence model, not by claiming that any individual heuristic is novel.

1. **One pipeline, two audiences.** Gmail and `.eml` become the same `NormalizedEmail`; protection and investigator views use the same case.
2. **Trust contradiction analysis.** Sender, Reply-To, Return-Path, organization claims, authentication, and link/infrastructure domains are correlated deterministically.
3. **Progressive disclosure.** A normal user receives an understandable verdict and safe next step before SPF, IOC, ASN, or graph detail.
4. **Evidence-bounded AI.** AI explains verified findings, cites valid evidence IDs, and falls back safely when unavailable.
5. **Cross-email context.** Shared infrastructure, domains, senders, ASNs, and attachment hashes form explainable related-case clusters.
6. **Conservative safety.** The product recommends actions without silently mutating a mailbox.
7. **Honest demo mode.** Synthetic Gmail and mock intelligence are visible and never presented as live account/provider truth.

The differentiation is integration, provenance, and usable decision support—not a claim that identity contradiction or email graphing has never been attempted elsewhere.
